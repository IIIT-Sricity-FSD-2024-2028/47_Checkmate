## Domain Context and Terminology

### Overall Purpose of the Problem Statement

The purpose of resource, staff, and venue allocation is to ensure structured planning, smooth coordination, and reliable execution of events. Proper allocation requires understanding client objectives, participant profiles, event type, budget constraints, and operational dependencies. It helps in minimizing conflicts, avoiding miscommunication, and managing real-time uncertainties.

### Primary Goals / Outcomes

- Ensure timely availability of staff, equipment, and venues  
- Reduce scheduling conflicts and dependency failures  
- Improve inter-department coordination  
- Support buffer and contingency planning  
- Maintain accountability and transparency  
- Enable effective crisis handling

## Core Workflows

### Workflow 1: Event Planning and Preparation

- Trigger: Client confirms event requirements  
- Steps:
  1. Collect detailed requirements (20–30 questions)  
  2. Analyze event type and participant profile  
  3. Identify involved staff and vendors  
  4. Allocate roles using 3W1H model  
  5. Book venue and resources  
  6. Prepare buffer and contingency plans  
- Outcome: Comprehensive event execution plan finalized  

---

### Workflow 2: Event Execution

- Trigger: Start of event day  
- Steps:
  1. Crew arrival 2–3 hours early  
  2. Equipment installation and testing  
  3. Venue setup and seating arrangement  
  4. Technical rehearsal  
  5. Live monitoring by Program Director  
  6. Time-based execution of activities  
- Outcome: Event conducted as per schedule  

---

### Workflow 3: Issue Handling and Recovery

- Trigger: Equipment failure, weather issues, staff absence, government restrictions  
- Steps:
  1. Identify and assess impact  
  2. Inform Program Director and team heads  
  3. Communicate with vendors and clients  
  4. Apply contingency measures  
  5. Document actions through official channels  
- Outcome: Service continuity and client trust maintained

## Rules, Constraints, and Exceptions

### Mandatory Rules / Policies

- All allocations must be verified and documented  
- Safety and venue regulations must be followed  
- Contracts must include force majeure clauses  
- Communication must follow official channels  

### Constraints / Limitations

- Budget constraints  
- Limited skilled workforce  
- Venue infrastructure restrictions  
- Government regulations  
- Time limitations  

### Common Exceptions / Edge Cases

- Sudden venue unavailability  
- Weather disruptions  
- Client scope changes  
- Vendor withdrawal  

### Situations Where Things Usually Go Wrong

- Incomplete requirement gathering  
- Poor vendor coordination  
- Lack of contingency planning  
- Miscommunication during crises
